Assembly order

Build and test in this order

  1. Bench-test board + cameraFlash the OS (Raspberry Pi OS Lite for A, Luckfox Buildroot for B). Record a 1-minute 1080p clip before soldering anything.
  2. Build the power stage aloneBattery → charge/boost module. Measure 5.0–5.2 V at the output under load with a multimeter. Only then connect the board.
  3. Add mic and gyroscopeShort wires, soldered straight to the pads. Check the mic records and the gyroscope reports on I2C.
  4. Add button, LED and motor driverTap the button: LED blinks, motor buzzes, a timestamp lands in events.jsonl.
  5. 30-minute heat test on the benchRecord continuously. If the board gets too hot to touch, add a small heatsink and vent holes in the case.
  6. Fit into the caseCamera and mic against the front holes with foam tape. Gyroscope glued rigid. Battery at the back, wrapped in Kapton tape.
  7. Close and attach the clipMagnet clip on the back. Check the lens sits level when worn on a shirt.
  8. 4-hour soak testWear it for a full function length. Note battery left, temperature, and any dropped files.

LiPo safety

  • Use only batteries with a protection circuit, and charge only through the charge module.
  • Never puncture, bend or short the pouch. Insulate wires and the pouch with Kapton tape.
  • Don't charge unattended, and stop using any battery that swells.
